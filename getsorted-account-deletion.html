GetSorted Account & Data Deletion

GetSorted™ is a home-inventory and organization app offered by BrightCasa LLC. Use this page to request deletion of your GetSorted account and associated data.

1. Start from the app

In the updated app, Delete Account opens this page. You must verify your email and explicitly confirm before deletion starts. Opening this page does not delete anything.

Open GetSorted and go to:
Settings → Account → Delete Account

2. Request your confirmation link

Enter the email address associated with your GetSorted account. We’ll send a short-lived confirmation link if an account exists for that address. If you already confirmed, a new link lets you check the saved request.

We only use this address to send the confirmation link.

If an account exists for that email address, we’ll send a confirmation link with the next step.

Permanently delete your GetSorted account?

This action cannot be undone.

Confirming saves your deletion request and blocks further changes to your account. Completion may require a review of photo uploads or shared Property ownership. Your account is not deleted until completion is confirmed.

What deletion removes

When deletion completes, GetSorted removes your account and its exclusively owned active data, including:

  • Account and profile information
  • Authentication data
  • Rooms
  • Containers
  • Inventory items
  • Categories
  • Scan and review-session records
  • Password-reset and temporary email-confirmation records
  • Uploaded GetSorted photos that belong exclusively to your account

Another person’s account and inventory they remain entitled to access are not included. Shared Property ownership or shared photos require a decision before completion. This process does not cancel a subscription.

Data that may remain temporarily

Exclusively owned active account data is deleted when the request completes. A minimal deletion-status receipt remains so the confirmed request is not lost and completion can be checked. Limited copies may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, delivery records, caches, or service-provider systems until their normal retention periods expire. These copies are not used to restore your account. Information may also be retained when required by law.
