GetSorted Account & Data Deletion

GetSorted™ is a home-inventory and organization app offered by BrightCasa LLC. Use this page to request deletion of your GetSorted account and associated data.

1. Delete your account in the app

The fastest way to delete your GetSorted account is from inside the app.

Open GetSorted and go to:
Settings → Account → Delete Account

2. Delete without access to the app

If you cannot access the app, enter the email address associated with your GetSorted account. We’ll send a short-lived confirmation link if an account exists for that address.

We only use this address to send the confirmation link.

If an account exists for that email address, we’ll send a confirmation link with the next step.

Permanently delete your GetSorted account?

This action cannot be undone.

Your GetSorted account and active associated data will be permanently deleted.

What deletion removes

When deletion completes, GetSorted removes active data associated with your account, including:

  • Account and profile information
  • Authentication data
  • Rooms
  • Containers
  • Inventory items
  • Categories
  • Scan and review-session records
  • Password-reset and account-deletion request records, as applicable
  • Uploaded GetSorted photos that belong exclusively to your account

Data that may remain temporarily

Active account data is deleted when the request completes. Limited copies may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, delivery records, caches, or service-provider systems until their normal retention periods expire. These copies are not used to restore your account. Information may also be retained when required by law.

More information

For details on how GetSorted handles your information, see the GetSorted Privacy Policy. Questions? Contact support@brightcasa.co.
