GetSorted Moving™

Pack with confidence.

Know what’s in every box and exactly where it goes.

Coming Soon

Moving is easier when every box has a plan. GetSorted Moving™ is being designed to help you keep track of what you packed, where it came from, and where it should go at your new home — without turning packing into another complicated project.

Pack. Label. Find.

A simple, three-step way to stay in control of what’s inside every box.

Packing Room

Choose the room you’re packing from, so you always know where the box started.

Box

Create a box and add what’s going into it. You can photograph several items before packing, and GetSorted Moving is being designed to help identify and record what’s in the box.

Drop-off Room

Choose the room where the box should be placed at your new home, so unpacking has a clear plan.

A marker is enough.

No printed labels, no QR codes, no special equipment. GetSorted Moving is being designed to give each finished box a short code you can write directly on the outside with a marker.

1-K Box 1
Drop-off Room: Kitchen

Simple to read. Simple to write. Easy to find later.

Find the box before you open it.

Instead of opening box after box looking for one thing, GetSorted Moving is being designed to let you search what you packed and see which box it’s in.

Search Coffee maker Result Box 22 — Kitchen

Document the box in seconds — keep packing.

The photo step is being designed to stay out of your way. Photograph several items before they go into the box. The app is designed to suggest the separate items it can identify. You review and correct those suggestions while packing, and repeat with the next group of items as needed.

If you like, keep an optional final photo of the packed box as a reference.

Show movers where everything goes.

GetSorted Moving is being designed to create a simple placement guide organized by Drop-off Room, so movers can see where finished boxes and larger non-boxed items belong — without seeing the private details of what’s inside.

Furniture and larger items, too.

GetSorted Moving is also being designed for things that don’t go into boxes — such as furniture and other large items — so their Packing Room and Drop-off Room can be recorded alongside everything else.

Already use GetSorted™?

GetSorted Moving™ is being designed as a separate companion app for the moving process. Over time, it can work with your existing GetSorted home inventory while keeping the everyday GetSorted experience simple.
