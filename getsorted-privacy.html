GetSorted™ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

1. Who We Are

GetSorted™ is a home-inventory and organization app offered by BrightCasa LLC ("BrightCasa," "we," "us," or "our").

2. Information You Provide

When you use GetSorted, you may provide us with:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and authentication information.
  • Home-inventory information such as rooms or areas, containers, items, names, descriptions, notes, categories, tags, brand, model, part number, serial number, quantity, expiration date, and other inventory details you choose to add.
  • Photos of rooms, containers, items, or related review content.
  • Password-reset information when you request a password reset.

3. Information Generated Through Use

We also generate or receive information needed to operate the service, including:

  • Internal user identifiers and record identifiers.
  • Timestamps and operational metadata.
  • Inventory and photo-review records created while you organize your home inventory.
  • AI-assisted recognition and suggestion results.
  • Review-session and photo-processing data used while you capture, review, and organize inventory.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain sessions.
  • Provide the GetSorted service and store your inventory.
  • Process photos and support photo-assisted organization.
  • Generate AI-assisted suggestions for names, categories, descriptions, and related organization tasks.
  • Enable search, retrieval, and management of your inventory.
  • Send password-reset emails.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.

Based on the current implementation, we do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

5. Photos and AI Analysis

GetSorted lets you submit photos to help organize household items.

Photos and image data may be transmitted to service providers that help us host, process, or analyze those images. GetSorted currently uses Cloudinary for image hosting and delivery and an AI integration service using an underlying AI model provider for AI-assisted image analysis.

AI-generated suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate. You should review AI-assisted suggestions before relying on them.

6. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to operate GetSorted, including:

  • MongoDB for database storage.
  • Cloudinary for image upload, hosting, and delivery.
  • AI integration and underlying model providers for AI-assisted image analysis.
  • Resend for password-reset email delivery when enabled.

These service providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

7. Data Sharing

We share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate GetSorted, maintain the app, or provide requested features such as data storage, image delivery, AI assistance, and password reset.

Based on the current implementation, GetSorted does not sell user data and does not use user information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate GetSorted, provide requested features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security and reliability.

Different types of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose and applicable legal requirements.

Information processed by third-party service providers may also be subject to those providers' retention requirements and practices.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your GetSorted account from within the app by going to:

Settings > Account > Delete Account

When you confirm account deletion, GetSorted deletes the account and associated user-owned inventory records from the application database and clears the authenticated session.

As part of the account-deletion process, GetSorted also requests deletion of associated remotely hosted Cloudinary photo assets identified by the account's stored photo records.

Account deletion depends on the availability and successful response of the services involved in completing the deletion process. GetSorted is designed to avoid reporting account deletion as complete when required remote photo cleanup fails.

Deleting an individual item, container, room, or photo outside the full account-deletion process may not independently remove every remotely hosted image associated with that record.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

GetSorted is a general consumer home-inventory application and is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner inconsistent with applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and provide additional notice where appropriate.
