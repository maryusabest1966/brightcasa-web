NoteRobin Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · NoteRobin 0.1.0 beta

NoteRobin is a personal memory-aid app from BrightCasa LLC. This page describes the public beta and the optional Text to Note test for invited testers. The BrightCasa website Privacy Policy applies when you visit our website or contact us.

Your personal notebook

People, About descriptions, notes, tags, selected contact details and unfinished edits are saved in the app’s local storage. This beta has no NoteRobin account or BrightCasa notebook server. It does not upload your notebook to BrightCasa, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or sell notebook data.

The app uses Apple’s built-in file protection. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data depending on your settings. The personal notebook is separate from the optional Shared Notebooks beta described below. CSV imports and exports are copies of the information you choose; they do not keep two notebooks continuously in sync.

Optional Shared Notebooks beta

Invited builds can use your existing Apple Account and Apple’s CloudKit service to share a notebook with people you invite. The notebook’s people, notes, tags, dates and the display name you supply are stored through Apple and available to authorized participants according to their access permissions. Participants with edit access can change shared content. You do not need a separate NoteRobin account.

Your personal notebook is not automatically included in a shared notebook. Exporting a shared notebook and importing it into your personal notebook creates a separate copy. Revoking sharing access does not recall exports or other copies someone has already saved.

Contacts you choose

When you use the iPhone Contacts picker, NoteRobin copies the names, phone numbers, addresses and contact identifiers provided for the people you select. It does not import the whole address book automatically. These copies remain in NoteRobin until you edit or delete them; they are not continuously synced with Contacts.

Adding a person to iPhone Contacts opens Apple’s contact form for you to review and save. NoteRobin notes and About text are not included. Calls and texts use your iPhone’s system apps and are handled by Apple and your carrier.

Optional Text to Note beta

Invited testers can verify a U.S. mobile number and send notes by SMS. BrightCasa uses Twilio to deliver verification codes and receive the texts you send to the NoteRobin number. This involves your mobile number, message contents and delivery details. We use this information to verify your phone, route texts to your installation and deliver them to your notebook.

New texts are available in the app inbox for up to two days. After the app saves or discards a text, it asks Twilio to remove the message body from its active logs. Twilio may retain delivery records, texts that have not been processed and copies in backups under its own retention practices. Use fictional names and sample notes while testing. Twilio’s message storage information

You can disconnect your phone in NoteRobin under Settings → Text to Note. Disconnecting stops delivery to your app. Notes already saved remain in your notebook until you delete them. To request help with information sent through the beta, email hello@brightcasa.co.

Optional AI interpretation of texts

We are preparing AI interpretation for an invited private beta. If your build offers it, AI starts off. Enabling “Understand natural-language texts” lets NoteRobin send the content of a text you open for review to Emergent’s Universal Key service and OpenAI’s GPT-4o Mini model. The purpose is to extract the person’s name, note and any tags you requested, so you can write a natural sentence instead of using separate lines.

The AI request includes the text’s full content, including any names or private details you wrote. It does not include your phone number, Contacts list, notebook contents or counseling history as separate context. Twilio still handles the phone number and message as described above. The AI does not choose who may access your shared notebook. You review the notebook, person, note and tags before saving, and can correct the extracted text.

The service temporarily caches an interpretation in Twilio Sync for up to two days to avoid repeated paid requests. After you save or discard the text, the app requests removal of that cached interpretation and the active SMS body. Saved notes and unfinished review edits remain on your device, or in the shared notebook you choose. Turning AI off prevents new interpretation requests; it does not recall content already sent to a provider.

Emergent and OpenAI handle requests under their own data practices. Provider logs or backups may remain after NoteRobin clears its temporary copy. See Emergent’s privacy information and OpenAI’s API data controls. This beta does not promise zero provider retention. Use fictional notes while testing.

Dictation

Dictation asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. The app requires on-device speech recognition; when that is unavailable, you can keep typing. It processes microphone audio while you dictate and saves the resulting text, not an audio recording. It does not send dictation audio to a BrightCasa service. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

PDFs, mailing labels and sharing

Exports can include the people, About text, tags, notes or mailing addresses you select. When you save, print or share an export, the destination you choose receives that information under its own privacy practices. NoteRobin creates temporary PDF files on your device for preview and sharing. Copies you save or share elsewhere are separate from your notebook and must be managed at those destinations.

Deletion and retention

People deleted in NoteRobin move with their notes to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, unless you permanently delete them sooner. Deleting in NoteRobin does not delete the original person from iPhone Contacts. Deleting a reusable tag removes its assignments, not the people or notes. Device backups, saved exports and information you have already shared can remain after an in-app deletion.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple distributes this beta through TestFlight. Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with BrightCasa. Feedback you submit is also shared with us under TestFlight’s privacy practices. That is separate from uploading your notebook. Feedback you choose to send may contain screenshots or other personal information, so review it before sending.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use support and beta feedback to respond and improve the app, and retain it as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Please avoid sending private details about other people.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@brightcasa.co with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to BrightCasa. We cannot access or recover your local notebook through a NoteRobin account. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
