NoteRobin Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · NoteRobin 0.1.0 beta

NoteRobin is a personal memory-aid app from BrightCasa LLC. This page describes the current beta. The BrightCasa website Privacy Policy applies when you visit our website or contact us.

Your notebook stays on your device

People, About descriptions, notes, tags, selected contact details and unfinished edits are saved in the app’s local storage. This beta has no NoteRobin account or BrightCasa notebook server. It does not upload your notebook to BrightCasa, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or sell notebook data.

The app uses Apple’s built-in file protection. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data depending on your settings. This beta does not provide its own iCloud sync or complete backup/import feature.

Contacts you choose

When you use the iPhone Contacts picker, NoteRobin copies the names, phone numbers, addresses and contact identifiers provided for the people you select. It does not import the whole address book automatically. These copies remain in NoteRobin until you edit or delete them; they are not continuously synced with Contacts.

Adding a person to iPhone Contacts opens Apple’s contact form for you to review and save. NoteRobin notes and About text are not included. Calls and texts use your iPhone’s system apps and are handled by Apple and your carrier.

Dictation

Dictation asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. The app requires on-device speech recognition; when that is unavailable, you can keep typing. It processes microphone audio while you dictate and saves the resulting text, not an audio recording. It does not send dictation audio to a BrightCasa service. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

PDFs, mailing labels and sharing

Exports can include the people, About text, tags, notes or mailing addresses you select. When you save, print or share an export, the destination you choose receives that information under its own privacy practices. NoteRobin creates temporary PDF files on your device for preview and sharing. Copies you save or share elsewhere are separate from your notebook and must be managed at those destinations.

Deletion and retention

People deleted in NoteRobin move with their notes to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, unless you permanently delete them sooner. Deleting in NoteRobin does not delete the original person from iPhone Contacts. Deleting a reusable tag removes its assignments, not the people or notes. Device backups, saved exports and information you have already shared can remain after an in-app deletion.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple distributes this beta through TestFlight. Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with BrightCasa. Feedback you submit is also shared with us under TestFlight’s privacy practices. That is separate from uploading your notebook. Feedback you choose to send may contain screenshots or other personal information, so review it before sending.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use support and beta feedback to respond and improve the app, and retain it as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Please avoid sending private details about other people.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@brightcasa.co with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to BrightCasa. We cannot access or recover your local notebook through a NoteRobin account. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
