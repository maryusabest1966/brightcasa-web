NoteRobin™

Remember the people. Remember the details.

A simple place for the little things you want to remember about the people in your life. Write a quick note, dictate a thought, or find someone by the tags that connect you.

The iPhone beta is opening soon

We’re preparing public testing through Apple’s TestFlight app. The signup link will appear here once the beta is ready.

Public signup is not available yet.

iOS 17 or later. This is an early beta; features may change. Keep a separate copy of information you cannot replace.

A little context goes a long way

  • Start with a person or a description. Add someone from iPhone Contacts, or write how you know them and add their name later.
  • Type or dictate. Save notes and About details, then search names, descriptions, notes and tags.
  • Find your groups. Filter people by shared tags and keep your selections while adding someone else.
  • Keep contact details handy. Store phone numbers and addresses, with calling and texting through your iPhone.
  • Make something useful. Export selected people and notes to PDF, print an address checklist, or create Avery 5160 / 8160 mailing labels.

Simple, with no NoteRobin account

This beta saves your notebook on your device. It has no NoteRobin account, ads, or in-app subscription. App Lock, reminders, iCloud sync and an in-app backup/import feature are not available in this beta.

Apple may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings. Saving a PDF is an export, not a complete notebook backup. Avoid uninstalling the app to troubleshoot.
